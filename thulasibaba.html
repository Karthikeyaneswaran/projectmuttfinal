Sri Thulasibaba Tradition

The spiritual teachings of Rama Namam and the historic relics of the mutt.

Rama Nama Satsangam

At the heart of the Mutt's spiritual practices is the continuous chanting of the "Ram" Taraka Mantra. The name of Rama holds infinite power to transform lives, as exemplified by Sage Valmiki, who transformed from a hunter to a sage through constant chanting, and Sri Panchamukha Hanuman, whose strength flows from Rama's name.

Our daily satsangams and bhajans invite all devotees to join. Collective chanting purifies the environment and invites the protective grace of Lord Hanuman and Lord Rama.

Miraculous Relics of 1964

During the devastating Dhanushkodi cyclone of 1964, ancient idols of Lord Rama and Mother Sita were buried under the sand. By divine grace, they were safely retrieved and are now preserved in the Mutt. Devotees can also witness and worship the sacred 'Floating Stone' retrieved from Sethu, used by Sri Rama's army to bridge the ocean.
